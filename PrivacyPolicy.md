## 1. Introduction & Overview
GameBoard ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with applicable global privacy standards (including GDPR and CCPA). This policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, and protection.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
1. **User-Provided Data:**
   - **Account Credentials:** Username, email address, and password (stored using secure cryptographic hashing such as Argon2).
   - **Profile Information:** Optional bio and gender preferences.
   - **Chat & Live Audio:** Text messages in game rooms. Real-time voice chat operates via WebRTC / LiveKit. **Live voice communications are transmitted in real time and are never recorded or stored on our servers.**
2. **Technical Data:**
   - IP address (processed temporarily for anti-abuse and security purposes), unique device identifiers, operating system version, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for push notifications.
3. **Local Storage:**
   - Audio and interface preferences stored locally on your device.

---

## 3. Application Permissions
- **Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Enables real-time in-game voice chat. It is never accessed in the background and audio is never recorded.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used for game room invites and service alerts.
- **Internet Access:** Required for server connectivity and multiplayer gameplay.

---

## 4. Account & Data Deletion
Users may request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time:
1. **In-App:** Via **Menu → Profile → Delete Account**.
2. **Email:** Submit a request to `muamal.aljanahi@gmail.com` with the subject line **"Account Deletion Request"**.

**Retention Period:** Account credentials and profile data are permanently purged from active databases within **30 days**. Security logs are deleted after 12 months.

- Users retain rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port their personal data under applicable law.
- We do **not** sell personal data.
- The Service is intended for users aged 13 and older.
